Overview

These terms apply to every photo session, booking and online gallery with MH Captures/Media, a Highcrest Group company, based in Ontario, Canada. By booking a session or paying a deposit, you agree to them.

MH Captures/Media provides digital photography for events, portraits, family, grad and sports sessions. Questions about these terms go to mhmedia@mhcapturesmedia.com.

Booking, deposits and payment

A session is confirmed once the $30 deposit is paid online by card through Stripe. The deposit is applied to your session total.

The remaining balance is due 7 days before your session. Sessions with an unpaid balance may be rescheduled or cancelled at the photographer's discretion.

Prices on the website are in Canadian dollars. A travel fee may apply for sessions outside the London, Ontario area and is quoted before you book.

Cancellations, rescheduling and refunds

Cancel 7 or more days before your session for a full refund of the deposit and any amount paid.

Cancel less than 7 days before and the $30 deposit is kept. Late cancellations and no-shows forfeit any balance already paid, unless the photographer agrees otherwise.

One reschedule is free with at least 48 hours' notice. The new date is set by the photographer based on his availability, and further reschedules are at his discretion.

Refunds go back to the original card and may take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

Weather and location

If weather makes an outdoor session unsafe or unworkable, we reschedule at no charge when either side gives 48 hours' notice. Weather reschedules never cost a new deposit.

Clients are responsible for any permits or permission needed at a chosen location. We may move a session to a safer or better-lit spot, and we may end it early if conditions become unsafe.

Photo delivery and private gallery

We aim to deliver your edited photos within 48 hours of your session, but this is a goal, not a guarantee, and delivery may take longer. Photos are delivered through a private online gallery you log into on mhcapturesmedia.com. All delivery is digital; no prints or physical media are included.

The number of edited images depends on the session you booked, for example 10 edited images for a Winter Mini. Unedited photos are not delivered.

Your gallery stays online for a limited time, so download your photos promptly and keep your own backup. Your login is personal and should not be shared.

Photo release and minors
